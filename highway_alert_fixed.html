🚗 Highway Alert System — V2V Broadcast (Mini Project)

One vehicle detects a hazard → alert is broadcast to every vehicle behind it • Sudden stop • Road damage • Accident • Blind person • Drunk driver • Bikes

Controls




Click a vehicle in the list to see the road from that car. Keys ← → change lane.

Vehicles on the road

How other vehicles get the alert

  1. The vehicle that detects the event (hard braking, pothole sensor, crash sensor) sends a message.
  2. The message travels by V2V radio / mobile network (cloud server, like Google Maps).
  3. Every vehicle within 700 m behind the hazard receives it in about 0.25 s and slows down early.
